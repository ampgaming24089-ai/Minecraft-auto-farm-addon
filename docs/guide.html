Getting In

Craft Soulfire and Steel from one Soul Sand and one Iron Ingot. Build a frame out of Gold Blocks exactly the way you would build a nether portal - the corners are optional - and TAP the frame with the igniter. One tap is enough; you do not need to hold. The portal burns red. Walk through. If it will not light, the igniter tells you why: which blocks are missing, or that the opening is the wrong shape or size. Any rectangle from 2x3 up to 21x21 works. If it still will not light, nothing here depends on it. Open this guide and press 'Travel to the Hollow Veil' at the bottom, or type /scriptevent hollowveil:portal to have a working portal built where you stand.

Getting Home

Stand in the portal you arrived through, or in the pre-lit one beside Hollow Hamlet. You come out where you left.

The Five Regions

The Veil is a disc 100,000 blocks in every direction from the hub. Terrain builds itself around you as you travel, so there is no edge to reach quickly and nothing to load in advance.

The Misty Reach

Where you arrive, and where Hollow Hamlet stands. Lit, and nothing hostile spawns within 32 blocks of the shrine.

Lives here: Soul Wisp, Wraith.

The Grave Moors

Headstones to the horizon. The signature look of the dimension, and the ghosts' home ground.

Lives here: Wraith, Banshee, Shade, Poltergeist, Fallen Knight, Soul Wisp.

The Ashlands

Burnt red country. Demons, the deepest ore, and the only place wild dragons fly.

Lives here: Hellhound, Imp, Ashen Whelp, Bastion Sentinel, Veil Dragon.

The Boneyard Marsh

Wet, luminous, and the only place with anything worth eating.

Lives here: Bonehide Elk, Glimmershroom Toad, Marrow Crawler, Ashwing Bat.

The Sunken Ruins

A drowned city under guard. Bring something that shoots back.

Lives here: City Wraithguard, Ashwing Bat, Shade, Fallen Knight.

Armour Set Bonuses

Wear all four pieces of a set to get its bonus. Minecraft caps armour's damage reduction at 20 points - netherite is exactly 20 and every set here is above it - so these bonuses are where the tiering above netherite actually lives.

Wraithsteel

none. It is the entry suit; its 24 points are the point.

Veilsteel

Resistance I.

Hollowforged

Resistance I, Haste I, permanent fire resistance, and

Mourner's Shroud

permanent Speed I, Speed II below 30% health, and fall

Spectral Regalia

Resistance I, plus invisibility and water breathing

Ashen Demonplate

Resistance II and permanent fire resistance. The tank.

The Three Wardens

The Hollow King

Sunken Crypt. Health: 200.

Sigil: 5x Soul Shard, 3x Spectral Dust, 1x Paper.

Use the sigil on a Ritual Altar. The altar clears an arena around itself, walls it in and leaves a doorway, then the boss walks out.

Three phases, at full health, 66% and 30%. Each phase knocks the arena back, summons adds and speeds the boss up.

Drops: Hollow King's Reaper + Spectral Regalia.

Re-fightable: craft another sigil once the altar has settled (20 minutes).

The Veil Dragon

Taming and flying

Wild adults fly over the Ashlands, rarely. There is also a Veil Dragon Egg - boss loot, or buy one from the Occultist - which hatches a wild one wherever you use it.

Feed it: Ember Fruit (+8 temper), Veil Marrow Stew (+15 temper). Temper runs to 100; keep feeding until it stops throwing you.

Once tamed it can be ridden and named. It flies the way a Happy Ghast does - you steer by looking, and it climbs when you hold jump. Six colours, decided when it spawns.

The Occultist

Right-click the Occultist at the Hollow Hamlet shrine. The shop is a menu, not a vanilla trade screen: pick a category, pick a trade, and it checks your bag.
